Research Interests

My research focuses on understanding and modelling hydraulic and flood-related hazards, with an emphasis on practical approaches to dam safety and flood risk.

Flood Mapping & Risk

Research focused on flood mapping, flood hazard assessment, and understanding flood risk.

Dam Safety & Breach Assessment

Investigating dam safety and approaches to dam-breach analysis and downstream consequences.

Hydraulic Modelling

Application of hydraulic modelling to understand flow behaviour and support engineering analysis.

3D Point Clouds

Exploring cost-effective methods for generating 3D point clouds from 360-degree cameras for applications such as building documentation.
